# Whole Wheat Dinner Rolls

Who doesn't love dinner rolls?? Theyre so soft and pillowy, come pre-portioned, are visually stunning, and are super fun to make! Words cannot explain the satisfaction of ripping into a warm, steaming dinner roll with its beautiful golden top and fluffy interior. I was stoked to see [this recipe by The Woks of Life](https://thewoksoflife.com/whole-wheat-dinner-rolls/#recipe). Another way to use up my whole wheat flour! 

The results were as expected - absolutely phenomenal. They've outdone themselves once again. It was a hit with the entire family and a recipe I keep in my back pocket. Not only is it super affordable and accessible to make, it was super fun to make! I love being able to shape the dough into the rolls and getting that hands-on breadmaking fun. Not to mention, they are super forgiving. After all, who cares about dents and imperfections if you have steaming hot rolls in front of you?

They stay tasting fresh for days and reheat beautifully. These are great alternatives to Japanese milk bread rolls. Less of a sweet milk flavor and more of a deeper, nutty whole wheat flavor but just as soft and fluffy with the same easy method.

# Recipe

For the most part, I stuck to the [recipe](https://thewoksoflife.com/whole-wheat-dinner-rolls/#recipe). I made a few adjustments so admittedly, I'm not quite sure the full potential of the original recipe. My adjustments resulted in a gorgeous end result so it works for me :)  

I use the bread machine to mix the dough, making this even easier. For the normal 15 rolls (~460g flour), it proofed to full capacity already. So if you scale the recipe up, you might want to proof some of the dough in a separate container so it has enough to grow. 

Quantity: 22 rolls (scaled from 15 as per recipe)

- Tanzhong (50g whole wheat flour + 150g water)
- 21g yeast
- 625g whole wheat flour
- 100g water
- ~42g butter
- 50g honey (add more for sweeter rolls)
- 1.5 eggs
- 235ml milk
- 12g salt (~1.3tsp)
- 1 tbsp milk powder
- ~1.3tbsp vital wheat gluten 


1. Add everything except butter to bucket and mix until just combined
2. Run on dough cycle on bread machine. When the "add additions" indicator goes off, add the butter
3. 1st rise - proof until doubled in size
4. Punch down and shape into balls. 15 fit nicely in the 11inch springform pan. For 22, I used the 9inch and 10inch pan.
5. 30 min proof. Brush with remaining half an egg and bake at 350F until golden brown at centre rack (~25 minutes for me).

Additions:

- Hot cross buns (raisins, cranberries, cinnamon, cloves, nutmeg, ginger, orange zest)
- Chocolate
- Cheddar jalapeno
- Cinnamon swirl

After mixing cycle completed, remove some dough and add inclusions. Make sure to add inclusions aggressively! 

Note that some of the chocolate near the surface melts due to the warm environment. Shaping afterwards will be a bit messy.
